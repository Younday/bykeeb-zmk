Bykeeb

9 layers, one keyboard. Choose a layer to explore its bindings.

Tap the main label; hold for the green label. ▽ falls through to an underlying active layer. Hover a key to see its ZMK binding. Hold labels on the base layer show how to reach other layers.

Home-row modifiers: use the modifier on the opposite hand to your target key. After typing, pause briefly before a shortcut. Same-hand shortcuts need a deliberate hold. Tap then quickly hold a letter to repeat it.

Configured timings: Left: hold 280 ms, prior idle 150 ms, quick tap 175 ms; Right: hold 280 ms, prior idle 150 ms, quick tap 175 ms; Backspace/MEDIA: hold 200 ms, quick tap 200 ms; Left letter/NUM: hold 280 ms, prior idle 150 ms, quick tap 175 ms; Right letter/NUM: hold 280 ms, prior idle 150 ms, quick tap 175 ms; Sticky Shift: timeout 1500 ms, release on next press.

Combos (base key labels): Z + X → Ctrl+Z (default, colemak; chord window 50 ms; prior idle 150 ms); Enter + Space → Lock NUM right (default, colemak, numlft, numrht; chord window 50 ms); Tab + Space → Auto Numword (default, colemak, numword; chord window 50 ms).

Studio: Hold Bksp and press Q to unlock editing.

Base layout: Hold Bksp and press Z to select Colemak-DH. Hold Bksp and press X to select QWERTY. QWERTY is selected at startup. The diagram selector only changes this reference; use the keyboard shortcuts to switch the actual keyboard.

Numbers: the top inner letter on either half holds the opposite numpad. Enter + Space locks/unlocks the right numpad. Tab + Space toggles Numword, which exits on Space, Enter, Tab or other non-number input. Numeric thumbs are plain keys; Backspace repeats. Esc / exit NUM sends Escape and leaves numeric modes, keeping your chosen base layout. Lock and Numword combos are scoped to exclude each other's mode.

Editing: hold Enter for right-hand arrows and plain left-hand modifiers. Word/line selection uses Linux/Windows application shortcuts; Clear term line sends Ctrl+E, Ctrl+U. The outer right thumb is sticky Shift (or normal Shift while held); quote is on that thumb under SYM. Tap Backspace then quickly hold it for repetition; a normal hold still activates MEDIA.

This reference shows the firmware defaults. Changes saved in ZMK Studio are stored on the keyboard and are not reflected here. Use the SVG file for printing all layers.
